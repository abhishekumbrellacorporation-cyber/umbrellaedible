Membership Policies

Last Updated: October 2026 | UMBRELLAedible

1. Overview

UMBRELLAedible is a verified community of home cooks and professional chefs from India. By joining as a member, you agree to these policies.

2. Eligibility

  • Must be 16+ years old.
  • Must have a public Instagram account with food content.
  • Must be based in India or cooking Indian cuisine.
  • One account per person. Fake profiles will be removed.

3. Chef Verification Tiers

  • Home Cook: Passionate cooks, non-professional.
  • Professional Chef: Working in restaurants, cloud kitchens, or catering.
  • Founder Team: Core team selected by UMBRELLAedible. Cannot be requested.
  • Verified Badge: Blue tick given after manual Instagram + content review.

4. Recipe & Content Policy

  • All recipes must be original or with proper credit.
  • No copied content from YouTube, Instagram, or blogs without permission.
  • Photos must be your own. No stock images or AI images.
  • We reserve the right to edit titles for SEO or remove low-quality recipes.
  • You retain ownership, but grant UMBRELLAedible license to display it.

5. Instagram & Link Policy

Instagram button links directly to your profile. If your Instagram is found to be fake, inactive for 90+ days, or contains non-food spam, your membership may be paused.

6. Code of Conduct

  • No hate speech, abuse, or harassment towards other chefs.
  • No promotion of alcohol, tobacco, or illegal substances in recipes.
  • No spam, paid promotions, or MLM links in profile.
  • Respect community: No stealing recipes from other UMBRELLAedible chefs.

7. Membership Fee

Currently, membership is 100% Free. In future, we may introduce premium features with 30 days prior notice.

8. Termination & Removal

We can remove your profile if: you violate policies, receive 3+ valid reports, provide false information, or your Instagram is banned. You can request deletion anytime at: umbrellaedible@gmail.com

9. Privacy Policy

We only store: Name, Instagram handle, Chef Bio, and Recipes. We never share your email or phone publicly. Data is stored on Firebase (Google). You can request data deletion.

10. Disclaimer

UMBRELLAedible is a listing platform. We do not guarantee orders, earnings, or fame. Recipes are user-generated; please verify allergies and safety before cooking.

11. Changes to Policy

Policies may be updated. Continued use after update means you accept new policies. Major changes will be announced on Instagram.

12. Contact

For any query: umbrellaedible@gmail.com
Instagram: @umbrellaedible
